Claude Youtube Connector

A personal publishing tool for the Calming Rain Sounds YouTube channel.

The application prepares rain ambience videos, creates thumbnails and uploads them to the channel through GitHub Actions. It is operated by the channel owner and is not a public sign-in service.

Why it connects to Google

Google authorization lets the application confirm the intended YouTube channel, upload videos and thumbnails, and check whether uploads have finished processing. The owner chooses what is published and its visibility.

Owner control

The owner can stop the scheduled workflow or revoke the application's access through their Google account connections. Revocation does not remove videos already published.

Read the privacy policy for how authorization and upload records are handled.
